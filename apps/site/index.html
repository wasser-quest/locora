Remember what you want to keep.

Locora uses the method of loci: you place what you want to remember at places you know well, and later walk the route in your mind to recall it.

About

Locora is a memory palace app. You build palaces from pictures or videos, place your memories at spots in them, and review them with spaced repetition, so you revisit each one just before you would forget it.

How it works

  1. Choose a picture or video as a room.
  2. Place loci where you want to remember something.
  3. Walk the route and recall what each place holds.
  4. Review when the app says it is due.
