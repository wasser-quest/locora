The method of loci, made simple

Remember what you want to keep.

Turn a photo or video of a place you know into a memory palace. Put what you want to learn at its spots, walk the route in your mind, and Locora brings each item back just before you would forget it.

Open the web app Download

Free and open source (MIT). Works offline, no account needed. No ads, no tracking.

How it works

  1. 1

    Pick a place you know

    A photo of your flat or a video of your way to work becomes a palace. Add more rooms whenever you like.

  2. 2

    Place the loci

    Tap a spot, type what belongs there and a short memory aid. A few clicks per locus.

  3. 3

    Walk the route

    Start a journey with one click. Recall what each spot holds, reveal it, and grade yourself.

  4. 4

    Review when it is due

    Spaced repetition (FSRS) schedules every locus, in the order of your route.

What Locora does

  • Palaces from your own pictures

    Photos and videos of your own places, with several rooms per palace. In a video palace the film stops at each locus and goes on once you have recalled it.

  • Sound at every locus

    Give a locus its own audio clip. It plays when you reveal the answer.

  • Phone recordings as they are

    Videos and sound keep the codec they were recorded in, so adding them is fast and loses no quality. Location and other metadata are removed.

  • Journeys with FSRS

    Walk a palace, grade each answer, and let the FSRS scheduler bring back what is due.

  • A guided start

    In the app tour Simo builds an example palace in front of you and walks its route with you. An ⓘ next to fields and settings explains what each one is for.

  • Achievements

    For each lesson, the finished app tour and your first own palace, next to XP and trophies. All of it works offline.

  • Five languages

    English, Deutsch, Français, 日本語 and 中文, chosen on the first start.

  • Offline-first and open

    Your palaces live on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking. The whole app is open source under the MIT license.

Learn mnemonics with Simo and his friends

New in the app: 17 short lessons, from building vivid pictures to walking a route and repeating at the right time, plus history, research and memory sports. Every lesson ends with a small exercise and a quick check, and gives an achievement.

  • Simo

    The method of loci, walking a route, and its history. Named after Simonides of Ceos.

  • Mina

    Common mistakes and what research says. Named after Mnemosyne, the goddess of memory.

  • Cami

    How to picture things: vivid, absurd, moving, with all senses. Named after Giulio Camillo.

  • Bruno

    Palaces, places and routes, and how to reuse them. Named after Giordano Bruno.

  • Hafiza

    Numbers, repetition and memory sports. Her name means “memory” in Turkish.

Locora Cloud: sync between your devices

Locora works without an account. If you use it on several devices, the optional Locora Cloud keeps desktop, phone, browser and CLI in step.

  • End-to-end encrypted. Palaces, pictures, videos and sound are encrypted on your device with your own password before they are sent. The server cannot read them.
  • Automatic. Enter the encryption password once and Locora syncs on its own. A sync by hand first shows which palaces change.
  • Free for six months. Every new account can sync for six months at no cost. A subscription will follow later, on the web.
  • Your account in the browser. On web.locora.cloud you reset your password, see your storage, and delete your cloud data or your whole account.

Kurz auf Deutsch

Locora ist eine kostenlose, quelloffene App für die Loci-Methode. Aus Fotos und Videos deiner eigenen Orte werden Gedächtnispaläste; die Orte setzt du mit wenigen Klicks, und die Wiederholung (FSRS) holt jedes Element zurück, kurz bevor du es vergessen würdest. In „Mnemotechnik lernen“ erklären Simo und seine Freunde Mina, Cami, Bruno und Hafiza die Technik in 17 kurzen Lektionen mit Übungen.

Für Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iPhone/iPad, im Browser und als CLI. Offline, ohne Werbung, ohne Tracking. Die optionale Locora Cloud synchronisiert Ende-zu-Ende-verschlüsselt; neue Konten sind sechs Monate kostenlos.
