The method of loci, made simple

Remember what you want to keep.

Place what you want to learn at spots in a picture you know well. Later, walk the route in your mind and recall each one. Locora then reminds you just before you would forget.

Open the app See how it works

Works offline in your browser. No account needed to start.

How it works

  1. 1

    Choose a room

    A picture or a video of a place you know well: your home, your street, a station.

  2. 2

    Place the loci

    Put each thing you want to remember at a spot in the room, and add a short hint.

  3. 3

    Walk the route

    Go through the room in your mind and recall what each spot holds. Then check yourself.

  4. 4

    Review when it is due

    Spaced repetition brings each item back just before you would forget it.
