The first public release of Locora: memory palaces from your own photos and videos, offline first.

### Highlights
- **Workbooks in journeys:** tables and formulas feed loci, with typed input fields and actions after grading.
- **Import and export** of `.locora` files with their pictures and videos.
- **Settings and statistics:** new loci per day, target retention, and personal FSRS weights fitted from your own history.
- **Start screen:** a welcome page with Simo on the first start, plugin tiles (review plan, next due day) and a command palette (Ctrl/⌘+K).
- **Graphics:** tile icons, journey feedback and locus pictures as animated `.sply` graphics; zoom on a locus while asking.
- **Learning days** follow your local time, with an adjustable start hour.

### Fixes
- A percent sign always means percent in formulas.
- Animations never take the keyboard focus.

Downloads for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and the CLI are below; `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums.
