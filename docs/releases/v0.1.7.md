### New
- **XP that rewards remembering on time:** every locus earns XP once per learning day, whatever you grade it: 10 for a review that is due, 6 for a new locus, and 20 once a day when every due review is done. Practising before a locus is due is still welcome and earns nothing, so walking the same palace again and again no longer counts. Mistakes cost nothing: there are no hearts any more, and after a miss Locora says „Comes back soon – that is how it sticks.“
- **Today's goal is what is due:** the bar in the journey shows your XP, how many of today's due reviews are done and your week. With a big backlog the day counts as done after 50 reviews.
- **A weekly goal instead of a fragile streak:** choose on how many days a week you want to learn (1–7, default 4) in the settings. A learning day counts when you review or learn something on any of your devices, or when nothing is due. Locora shows your learning days, this week, and how many weeks in a row you reached your goal; a missed day costs nothing, and after a missed week it simply starts again.
- **Palace medals:** each palace card shows how many of its loci are consolidated (they hold for 30 days or more). Palaces with at least five loci earn the medals Anchored, Consolidated and Lasting when 9 in 10 of their loci hold for 7, 30 or 180 days – something only reviews spread over days can do.
- **Journey trophies as medals:** first journey, 100 XP, 7 learning days, every due review on one day and the weekly goal four times, each with the day you earned it, in Achievements next to the other medals. A new one is celebrated once, on the device where you earned it.
- **The end of a journey** says what you earned and what comes next: due reviews and new loci, what is still due today, new medals, and when the next review is.
- **The same progress on every device:** with Locora Cloud, your XP, learning days, trophies and medals are the same on all your devices, also after learning on two of them offline – nothing is lost and nothing counts twice. In the web app the game's progress is now part of what syncs. Your progress so far is taken over once.
- **Delete game progress:** Settings → Game progress & achievements deletes XP, trophies, medals, learning days and weekly runs on this device and, at their next sync, on all your devices; palaces, the review schedule and your settings stay. When you are signed in, your ranking entries on Locora Cloud are deleted too. CLI: `locora game reset`, `locora game status`, `locora game week-goal <days>`.

### Changed
- Gamification sends no notifications; only the reminders you turn on yourself remain.
- The „Stats“ page of the start screen is called „Progress“, in every language.
- Rankings in the CLI count ranking points.
- Plugin API 0.7: plugins can list what changed in their storage (`storage.list@1`, also changes from a sync) and hear about each answer with its learning day (`core.journey.reviewed`). Plugins built for 0.6 keep working.
- Devices with an older Locora version keep showing their old numbers and medals until they are updated.

### Downloads
Android ships as an APK, iPhone and iPad as an unsigned `.ipa` (AltStore or Sideloadly). The desktop installers are not code-signed yet: on macOS open the app with right-click → *Open*, on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*. The web app at [app.locora.cloud](https://app.locora.cloud) updates with this release. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums. To install with an AI assistant, paste `Install Locora. Follow https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wasser-quest/locora/main/INSTALL.md`.
