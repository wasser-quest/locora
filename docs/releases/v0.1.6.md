### New
- **XP that rewards remembering on time:** every locus earns XP once per learning day, whatever you grade it: 10 for a review that is due, 6 for a new locus, and 20 once a day when every due review is done. A review is due all day on the learning day it falls on, whether you learn in the morning or in the evening. Practising before a locus is due is still welcome and earns nothing, so walking the same palace again and again no longer counts. Mistakes cost nothing: there are no hearts any more, and after a miss Locora says „Comes back soon – that is how it sticks.“
- **Today's goal is what is due:** the bar in the journey shows your XP, how many of today's due reviews are done and your week. With a big backlog the day counts as done after 50 reviews. Loci you answered „Again“ come back later the same day for practice; they are shown as such and earn no second XP.
- **A weekly goal instead of a fragile streak:** choose on how many days a week you want to learn (1–7, default 4) in the settings. A learning day counts when you review something due or learn something new on any of your devices; just opening the app does not count. A day with nothing due lowers that week's goal by a day – Locora tells such days from your review schedule, so you need not open the app on them. Locora shows your learning days, this week, and how many weeks in a row you reached your goal; a missed day costs nothing, and after a missed week it simply starts again.
- **Palace medals:** each palace card shows how many of its loci are consolidated (they hold for 30 days or more). Palaces with at least five loci earn the medals Anchored, Consolidated and Lasting when 9 in 10 of their loci hold for 7, 30 or 180 days. Only reviews on different days count: a locus needs a right answer on a later day than its first, and walking a palace again and again in one sitting earns no medal – nor does it push its next review months away any more.
- **Journey trophies:** first journey, 100 XP, 7 learning days, every due review on one day and the weekly goal four times, each with the day you earned it, in Achievements next to the medals. A new one is celebrated once, on the device where you earned it; several earned at once are celebrated one after another.
- **The end of a journey** says what you earned and what comes next: due reviews and new loci, what is still due today, new trophies and medals, when the loci you practised early come back, and when the next review is.
- **The same progress on every device:** with Locora Cloud, your XP, learning days, trophies and medals are the same on all your devices, also after learning on two of them offline – nothing is lost and nothing counts twice. In the web app the game's progress is now part of what syncs. Your progress so far is taken over once.
- **Delete game progress:** Settings → Game progress & achievements deletes XP, trophies, palace medals, learning days and weekly runs on this device and, at their next sync, on all your devices; palaces, the review schedule, your lessons, the app tour and your settings stay. When you are signed in, your ranking entries on Locora Cloud are deleted too; if the server cannot do it right away, Locora asks again at the next start, sign-in or sync. CLI: `locora game reset`, `locora game status`, `locora game week-goal <days>`.
- **Manage friends in the CLI:** `locora cloud friend` lists your friends, the requests to you and the requests you sent, each with an id and the command that answers it. `locora cloud friend remove <id>` ends a friendship for both of you, `withdraw <id>` takes back a request you sent, and `decline <id>` says no to a request; further requests from someone you declined do not reach you until you ask them yourself.
- **Invite friends who are not on Locora yet:** a request to an address without an account waits as an invitation and reaches the person once they sign up and confirm that address.
- **Invite links in the CLI:** `locora cloud friend invite` makes a link, with a code such as `ABCD-EFGH-JKLM-NPQR`, that you can send any way you like. Whoever opens it sees who invites them, and once they accept you are friends; no e-mail address is needed. A link works once and for 14 days, and up to 10 can be open at a time. `locora cloud friend invite list` shows your open links, `locora cloud friend invite revoke <code>` withdraws one, and `locora cloud friend accept-invite <code or link>` accepts someone else's.
- **Report odd names:** rankings and challenge rankings in the CLI now show a reference next to each name, and in the friend list the id serves as one. `locora cloud report <reference>` reports a name as offensive or odd, and `--reason` adds a few words. Once several people with a confirmed e-mail address have reported a name, everyone else sees it as `Nutzer-` with a short code until it has been reviewed. You always see your own name.
- **Friends in the app and the web app:** Settings → Locora Cloud → Friends & rankings (also in the command palette, and beside the gear on the start page while your account has access) lists the requests to you, your friends and the requests you sent, with when you sent them. Accept a request by asking back with the person's address, decline it, withdraw your own, end a friendship, or ask someone new by e-mail address – the app never shows anyone's address. Every name can be reported, with an optional reason.
- **Invite links in the app:** make a link and share it or copy it, see your open links with their end date and withdraw them, also when your account has no access to friends any more. Paste a link or a code from a friend to see who invites you before you accept exactly that invitation. Send a link only to the person you want to invite: whoever accepts it first becomes your friend.
- **Rankings in the app:** the ranking of your friends and the one of everyone, with ranking points. They come from journeys Locora Cloud checks itself; journeys in the app do not count there yet, and the page says so.
- **Share achievements:** trophies, medals, a finished journey and a reached weekly goal become a square picture with their medal, name and date, plus a short text. Your name and palace names are on it only when you turn them on for that share. The web app hands it to your system's share sheet where it can share files, otherwise it downloads the picture; the desktop and phone apps save the picture. The text is copied either way. Nothing is sent to Locora Cloud.

### Changed
- Gamification sends no notifications; only the reminders you turn on yourself remain.
- Answering a locus again on the same day no longer raises its memory stability: practice in one sitting does not move its next review further away.
- The „Stats“ page of the start screen is called „Progress“, in every language.
- Rankings in the CLI count ranking points.
- Plugin API 0.7: plugins can list what changed in their storage (`storage.list@1`, also changes from a sync) and hear about each answer with its learning day (`core.journey.reviewed`). Plugins built for 0.6 keep working.
- Devices with an older Locora version keep showing their old numbers and medals until they are updated.
- **A safer key backup:** the app, the web app and the CLI now send a proof of your encryption key with the key backup. Locora Cloud keeps only a fingerprint of that proof and replaces the backup only for someone who holds the key, so a signed-in session alone (a stolen one, say) can no longer swap it for a backup you cannot open. A backup made with an older version gets this protection with the first sync after the update, which sends it back unchanged with the proof. Older versions that try to replace a protected backup are asked to update.
- **Friend requests reveal nobody:** asking for someone by e-mail address gives the same answer whether the address has an account or not, and their name appears only once you are friends. The requests you sent are listed with the date you sent them, without the address or a name. Unanswered requests and invitations expire after 30 days.
- **Confirm your e-mail address for the full storage:** until your address is confirmed, Locora Cloud keeps up to 64 MiB of pictures and videos and 32 MiB of synced changes for your account. What is stored already stays, but nothing new is added until you confirm; the app and the CLI then say that confirming your address gives you more space. Version 0.1.5 shows its general "storage is full" message in that case.
- **Unconfirmed accounts expire:** a free account whose address is not confirmed is deleted six months after it was registered, with all its data, even if it keeps syncing. The warning e-mail a month before says so. Confirm your address and sync once more, and the usual rule applies again: your data stays as long as you sync.
- **At most three accounts per inbox:** addresses that differ only in capitals, in a `+tag` before the `@` or, for Gmail, in dots count as one inbox, and Locora Cloud makes at most three accounts for it.
- **Names in the app are shown safely, too:** names from other people appear as plain text with the same visible escapes as in the CLI, and can never turn into markup or change the text around them.
- **Names in the CLI are shown safely:** display names and challenge names are chosen by other people. The CLI now prints control characters, text-direction overrides and other invisible characters as visible escapes such as `\u{202e}`, so a name can no longer pass itself off as something else or change your terminal. Emoji stay as they are, and overlong names are cut off.

### Downloads
Android ships as an APK, iPhone and iPad as an unsigned `.ipa` (AltStore or Sideloadly). The desktop installers are not code-signed yet: on macOS open the app with right-click → *Open*, on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*. The web app at [app.locora.cloud](https://app.locora.cloud) updates with this release. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums. To install with an AI assistant, paste `Install Locora. Follow https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wasser-quest/locora/main/INSTALL.md`.
