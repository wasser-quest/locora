### New
- **Choose your language on the first start:** English, Deutsch, Français, 日本語 or 中文, with your system's language first.
- **Start the app tour** from the welcome page: it highlights each control with a frame, keeps its panel off the stage and sets up the tutorial palace.
- **More rooms from pictures and videos:** “+ Room from a picture or video” next to the rooms adds one more room to a palace at any time.
- **Space confirms everything in a journey:** reveal, check a quiz, continue; on a revealed recall Space grades like 1.

### Changed
- **A calmer editor:** a three-step guide at the top, larger fields and buttons, and rarely needed settings (picture size, zoom) under “More settings”.
- Hints are no longer shown in red; only real problems are.
- The tutorial and the formula guide are replaced by a fresh copy when you switch the language.
- The chosen theme sets the page background; the activity grid keeps its squares together.
- Videos never flash an empty stage between questions.
- Animation rooms no longer show video buttons that did nothing there.

### Downloads
Android ships as an APK, iPhone and iPad as an unsigned `.ipa` (AltStore or Sideloadly). The desktop installers are not code-signed yet: on macOS open the app with right-click → *Open*, on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*. The web app at [app.locora.cloud](https://app.locora.cloud) updates with this release. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums.
