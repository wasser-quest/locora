### New
- **A new app tour with Simo:** in 13 short steps Simo builds an example palace in front of you. He turns a picture into a palace, places a locus on the door, and fills in the place, the item and a memory aid while the tour explains what each field is for. Then he walks the route with you: remember, reveal, grade. You can click and try everything during the tour; the page is only lightly dimmed.
- **Sound at every locus:** give a locus its own audio clip (MP3, M4A, WAV, Ogg, FLAC, WebM). It plays when you reveal the answer. Also in `.locora` files, sync and the CLI (`locora edit … --audio clip.m4a`).
- **Phone and browser recordings stay as they are:** iPhone videos (HEVC, MOV), Android recordings, AV1, VP9 and WebM are kept in their own codec, so adding them is fast and loses no quality. Only metadata such as location is removed. Rare formats (ProRes, AMR, …) are still converted. When a device cannot play a format, Locora says so instead of showing an empty stage.
- **More rooms from pictures and videos:** “+ Room from a picture or video” next to the rooms adds a room to a palace at any time.
- **Choose your language on the first start:** English, Deutsch, Français, 日本語 or 中文, with your system's language first.
- **Space confirms everything in a journey:** reveal, check a quiz, continue; on a revealed recall Space grades like 1.

### Changed
- **A calmer editor:** a three-step guide at the top, larger fields and buttons, and rarely needed settings (picture size, zoom) under “More settings”.
- Hints are no longer shown in red; only real problems are.
- A new palace opens with its picture in view.
- The video timeline lists a new locus at once.
- The tutorial and the formula guide are replaced by a fresh copy when you switch the language.
- The chosen theme sets the page background; the activity grid keeps its squares together.
- Videos never flash an empty stage between questions.

### Install with an AI assistant
Paste `Install Locora. Follow https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wasser-quest/locora/main/INSTALL.md` into your assistant. It installs the CLI, or the app as well when you ask for it.

### Downloads
Android ships as an APK, iPhone and iPad as an unsigned `.ipa` (AltStore or Sideloadly). The desktop installers are not code-signed yet: on macOS open the app with right-click → *Open*, on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*. The web app at [app.locora.cloud](https://app.locora.cloud) updates with this release. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums.
