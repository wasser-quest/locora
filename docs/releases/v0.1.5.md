### New
- **Learn mnemonics:** a new page with 17 short lessons, from building vivid pictures to walking a route and repeating at the right time, plus history, research and memory sports. Simo and his new friends **Mina, Cami, Bruno and Hafiza** explain them. Every lesson ends with a small exercise and a quick check, and each one gives an achievement.
- **A clear way in:** the welcome card shows four steps: learn the techniques, take the app tour, walk the tutorial palace, build your **first own palace**. The first own palace is guided: a photo of your room, five places, a shopping list, one walk along the route.
- **Before the tour** Mina asks whether you have practised the techniques yet. Finishing the tour gives the achievement „Tour completed“.
- **Automatic sync:** enter the encryption password once and Locora syncs on its own. The key is kept in the system's secure store (Keychain, Credential Manager, Secret Service, Android Keystore) or, in the browser, wrapped by a key the page cannot export. You can forget it on the device at any time.
- **See what a sync changes:** before a sync Locora lists the palaces that are new, changed or deleted. Deleting a palace on all your devices asks first.
- **Cloud accounts:** a reminder to confirm your e-mail address, „Forgot password?“ (reset on web.locora.cloud), and the recovery key can be saved as a file or copied.

### Changed
- **Much faster sync:** one connection instead of one per request, one check for all pictures instead of one each, transfers in parallel, and superseded changes are no longer sent. In our test with two palaces and a slow connection, a first sync went from 4.7 s to 1.2 s.
- Guest accounts sync during their six-month trial; „Freischaltung aktualisieren“ no longer asks for a subscription.
- On Android the keyboard no longer covers the app; the tour's panel stays visible, and the commands bar hides during the tour.
- On phones the welcome card fits the first screen.
- Many texts that were still German are translated: the journey summary, statistics, sync results, editor checks and the cloud's messages.

### Downloads
**No macOS or iPhone files in this release:** GitHub had no macOS build machines when it was made. On a Mac or iPhone, use the web app at [app.locora.cloud](https://app.locora.cloud) (it is on 0.1.5) or the 0.1.4 downloads; the next release has them again.

Android ships as an APK, iPhone and iPad as an unsigned `.ipa` (AltStore or Sideloadly). The desktop installers are not code-signed yet: on macOS open the app with right-click → *Open*, on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*. The web app at [app.locora.cloud](https://app.locora.cloud) updates with this release. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums. To install with an AI assistant, paste `Install Locora. Follow https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wasser-quest/locora/main/INSTALL.md`.
